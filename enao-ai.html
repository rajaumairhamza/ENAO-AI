ENAO AI — From the Heart

Every answer, searched for from the heart.

The whole internet, on your one question.

ENAO AI is not just a search machine. It is your companion — it understands every question, searches the whole internet, and answers in your own language, the way someone close would explain it to you.

  • Live internet search — real web sources from the ENAO search service, with Wikipedia and DuckDuckGo browser fallbacks (no paid API)
  • Mega Deep Research with NO quantity limit: ask for 1,000, 5,000 or 10,000 YouTube channels, websites or social media accounts on any topic — research runs batch by batch across the whole internet with live progress, everything found is analyzed, and the answer ends with a clean best-of-the-best ranking
  • Answers are written by a free Pollinations AI model; if it is unavailable, the answer is built from the sources' extracts
  • Voice uses the browser's built-in Web Speech API — lifetime free
  • Permanent memory: all chats and questions-answers stay saved in this browser — past conversations are remembered even after a reload or reopening, and a new question in an old chat is understood with its previous context
  • Live time memory: every conversation is also saved with its day, date, exact time and part of day (Morning / Afternoon / Evening / Night). The live clock at the top ticks every second, every message shows its time underneath, and before every answer the AI is given the current time plus the times of past conversations — so it knows when this talk is happening, in which part of the day, and can answer “when did we talk about this?” with the exact time
  • An answer to every question: a Roman Urdu question is first converted into English keywords for the search, and the answer comes back in Roman Urdu — the “I don't know” answer never comes
